# Grab & Drop AR

An augmented reality sandbox for Chrome on Android. Place realistic food and objects on real surfaces, grab them with your real hand (MediaPipe hand tracking), slice food, pour drinks, smash plates and knock over towers, pins and dominoes.

Uses WebXR hit-test, light estimation, depth sensing (occlusion and real-world collisions) and raw camera access when the phone supports them.

Open: https://johnkendall1994-cpu.github.io/grab-and-drop-ar/
