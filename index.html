Grab & Drop AR

Put food and objects on your real floor or table, pick them up, throw them, stack them and take a bite.

👆 Tap a surface to place the selected item
✋ Press & hold an item to pick it up, then move your phone to carry it
🫳 Let go to drop it, or flick your phone to throw
😋 Quick tap food to take a bite, or other items to poke them
Point at the floor or a table and move your phone slowly
Tap a surface: place the selected item
Press & hold an item: pick it up and carry it with your phone
Let go: drop it. Flick your phone as you let go to throw it
Quick tap food: take a bite (3 bites and it's gone)
Quick tap other items: poke them
🗑: trash mode, so tapping removes items
🧹: clear everything
Size: Real, Big, Giant or Tiny for new items

Tip: find the floor first, then point at a table to place things on it.
